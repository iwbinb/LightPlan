# App Review / TestFlight notes — draft, not submitted

LightPlan is a local-first sun and moon photography planning app. No login is required. The reviewer can choose the clearly labeled example location or manually select a place without granting location access. Today's basic information is available without purchase. A non-consumable StoreKit purchase unlocks planning for other dates, saved plans, place collections and the complete widget.

Restore Purchases is available in Settings and on the purchase screen. Reading/exporting existing plans does not require an active paid entitlement. There is no subscription, advertising SDK, external payment form, continuous background location or medical/safety navigation claim.

Astronomical times are model estimates for the selected location and destination time zone. They do not include terrain, buildings, clouds or a guarantee of visible sunlight. Maps and place search use system services and may need a connection.
