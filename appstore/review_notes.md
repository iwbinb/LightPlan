# App Review / TestFlight notes — draft, not submitted

LightPlan is a paid-upfront, local-first Sun and Moon photography planning app. Users purchase the app on the App Store before downloading. The installed app includes all planning features; there are no in-app purchases, subscriptions, paywalls or app accounts. No login credentials are required for review.

The reviewer may choose the clearly labeled example location or manually select a place without granting location permission. Open Map, select a future date, mark a subject and compare Sun/Moon alignment. Search a chosen range of 1–90 civil days with optional altitude/alignment and Moon illumination conditions; results include up to 60 shooting windows. Save a shooting plan. The saved plan retains its observer, subject, destination time zone and exact time. Add a note, reopen the plan and use its field brief to share the schedule or open the saved observer in Apple Maps. Plans, favorites, backups and the full widget are included in the app purchase.

Local reminders require notification permission only when requested by the user. A saved plan or a scheduled system request is not a guarantee of notification delivery. Backup import/export and field-brief sharing use the system interfaces; the user chooses the recipient. Reinstalling the app does not automatically restore local plans, so users can export/import their own backups.

Astronomical times and alignments are model estimates for the selected location and destination time zone. They do not include terrain, buildings, clouds, route accessibility or a guarantee of visible sunlight. Calculations and saved-plan management work offline with known coordinates/timezones; maps and place search use system services and may need a connection. The app does not require continuous background location or include an advertising/analytics SDK.
